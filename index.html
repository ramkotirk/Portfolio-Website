Chandrakumar is Dean of industry relations / TBI and Associate Professor in the department of Mechanical Engineering, School of Mechanical & Construction, Vel Tech Rangarajan Dr. Sagunthala R&D Institute of Science and Technology. A qualified engineering professional turned to technology business incubation by chance, helping entrepreneurs for over a decade. An experience of a decade in TBI and academia. Prior to Vel Tech TBI has teaching experience of over 3 years in mechanical engineering.
